1. ABCD — ASAPP: best future customer-support workflow candidate
Source: https://github.com/asappresearch/abcd
Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.239/
License evidence: https://github.com/asappresearch/abcd/blob/master/LICENSE
Observed repository license: MIT, ASAPP Research (2021). A separate explicit
data-specific license was not observed; do not invent one. Filename release
v1.1; immutable commit/hash not verified in this bounded research.
Download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/asappresearch/abcd/master/data/abcd_v1.1.json.gz
This URL is mutable and was not downloaded. Over 10K dialogues, 55 intents,
30 action options; original/delexicalized text, customer/agent/action turns,
next-step labels, guidelines and fictional workflow ontology. Useful for
action sequencing, but its company policies cannot become Freya bank policy.
Publisher describes fictional customers; original fields include names,
addresses and account/contact data. Prefer delexicalized derivatives and
preserve MIT notices when shipping repository material. Deferred: archive
ingestion and data-license scope merit a separate bounded implementation.

IMPLEMENTED RESULT
Only new integrations/customer-datasets/ and this research file were written.
Standard-library downloader/parser pins exact source and license-notice hashes
in sources.json; cap 20 MB/file; cache/raw payloads ignored locally. Fifty
scenarios (25/source), deterministic selection, provenance and separate source
facts/synthetic planning. Actual-data and malformed-input/hash/leakage tests
passed; reproducible summary and output hash are in validation.txt.
API values, consumer narratives and source utterances are not copied into
shared output. Correction/wait/polite/question cues are hashed lexical evidence,
not verified emotion, real interruption timing or expected bank behavior.
All oracle_scope=conversation_only, execution_status=not_executed;
synthetic expected_outputs=[] and policy_mapping=null. No supplied bank
brief/API justified policy mapping. These are planning artifacts, not native
builder scenarios or a claimed Freya benchmark. No hosted Freya simulation,
commit or push. Downloaded-source attribution and adaptation notices accompany
every generated scenario; no complete dataset is vendored for shipping.
