No current/previous gold frames or states enter input features. Source slot spans are not used for input redaction. All TRAIN service schemas are separately declared public metadata, not selected by current gold service annotations.
Intent/state/action labels and is_transactional metadata never grant actual bank permission, financial consent, policy authority or ledger truth.
Official dev/test are untouched. Internal holdouts derive from official TRAIN. Template/conversation components are not proven IID. Partial previews and full versions share ancestry. No evaluation outcomes influenced collection/labels and no models were trained here.
Regex/name scrubbing is heuristic, not a universal anonymity guarantee. Unrecoverable values remain unknown under explicit masks; raw values are not exported into processed targets. Source value hashes are target/bookkeeping lineage only and never input features.
