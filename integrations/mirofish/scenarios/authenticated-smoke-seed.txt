Synthetic integration test: earlier workshop opening

Maya Reed and Noah Vale are the only two fictional participants in this test.
They discuss whether a neighborhood workshop should open thirty minutes earlier
next Saturday. Maya prefers the earlier opening because it fits her work
schedule. Noah prefers the usual opening because he travels by bus. Maya asks
Noah whether an earlier bus would be possible; Noah explains that the current
bus schedule is fixed. Both remain polite and consider each other's view.

This is a synthetic social discussion, not a prediction about real people.
There is no bank, customer account, payment, private identity, or external
workflow. The simulation should contain only these two participant accounts.
