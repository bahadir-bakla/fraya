Oracle design:
  Every script states the final product-specific amount, date, refusal/payment claim,
  callback or transfer request and the response required at confirmation. Partial
  fragments, negation and yes-but never authorize the earlier terms. Corrections
  happen before recording and require a complete new read-back and clear yes.
  The callback recipient is explicitly a sibling without security answers or authority.
  gardas / gardaş is only an authored interjection. Conversation and confirmations
  stay in English; there is no expectation of real dialect or speech recognition.
  B heterogeneous decisions remain per product: promise/refused/paid and
  paid/dispute/refused. B-close uses the first non-paid note in account order.
  The human-transfer row explicitly withholds all promise consent and insists after
  the one permitted help-first question. B10 prohibits notes anywhere on that call.
