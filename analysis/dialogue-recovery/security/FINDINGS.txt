Root cause
The scalar security_value extractor returns a complete canonical DOB. The controller nevertheless requires security_fallback(raw) to rederive it. Its parse_date -> number_words treats a digit-by-digit year as an additive number, which then fails the minimum year check. No identity comparison is dispatched. security_cue detects years only with a four-digit regex, misleadingly requests a missing year, and increments its clarification counter until termination. Returning only the requested year also fails because the raw buffer retains the preceding complete-but-unrecognized answer. This is a controller representation error, not a bank mismatch or actual missing information.

Proposed source-general requirement (main guide owned by builder_guidance)
Separate current-answer qualification from normalization. Qualify the actual self-answer span and its complete required components; do not use question operands, third-party/quoted/negated/hypothetical spans, stale completed answers or unresolved corrections. Validate a canonical extracted DOB as a whole YYYY-MM-DD calendar value. Preserve that candidate unchanged for mandatory verify_identity when its current self-answer evidence is complete. A narrower raw parser's unsupported spoken year form must not by itself invalidate this candidate or consume the genuine wrong-answer budget. Recognized conflicting source evidence still blocks dispatch, and a canonical extractor value cannot invent a missing year. Support both four explicit spoken year digits and supported century-plus-year words without additive reinterpretation. Raw fallback is only a candidate producer, never identity authority. Existing same-question fragment scope, explicit replacement/restart behavior, clearing on consumed answers and field changes remain mandatory. Clarification wording must identify a genuinely absent component instead of claiming an unsupported complete year is absent. Count bank wrong answers only from the current documented bank comparison response; keep format/recovery budgets separate.

Related blend-13 change
26eadb0 removes mandatory word-year reparsing and says to submit canonical extracted DOB as is. It identifies the same failure family, but its literal 'ask for the year only when the extractor reports it missing' is too broad for the current source-evidence protections: an erroneous canonical value may accompany a partial, contradictory or third-party utterance. Retain the normalization insight, not unconditional trust in extractor completeness.
