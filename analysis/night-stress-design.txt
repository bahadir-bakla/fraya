ACTOR DESIGN
These are compound authored text-delivery challenges, not audio, ASR, accent,
latency or interruption certification. The actor gives only spoken text and
follows the original recipient/security/willingness decisions. Amount/date
repairs finish on canonical values; unrelated numbers are explicitly unrelated.
A question about an AI/scam/security procedure is answered before continuing;
it never grants pre-verification access or invents a bank contact number.
Hypothetical third-party assistance does not change who is actually on the line.

The A consent scenario can withhold permission at read-back and reconfirm
unchanged final values after a fresh read-back. The B consent scenario withdraws
provisional willingness BEFORE the single grouped read-back, then gives one
successful confirmation of all three final promises. No B scenario deliberately
requires multiple successful confirmations or multiple read-backs on a compliant
run. All B payment stress clauses are in one mandatory two-sentence aggregate
answer, so they do not depend on optional later product questions. A correction
chain is completed before the caller responds; it is not
an opportunity to record a temporary value. No scenario asks to change a promise
after it is recorded. Actual third-party, human transfer, hardship, dispute or
paid dispositions would change these official ledgers, so they are outside this
delivery-only pack. Other registered public fixtures stay in the parent's public
regression queue; existing authored stress catalogs already cover other paths.

Every extra check is tagged [ledger], [dialogue] or [actor]. Ledger assertions
state the exact rows and forbidden side effects; dialogue assertions bind opening,
security, product statements, date/amount scope, read-back, consent, successful
recording and closing to actual conversation evidence. Actor assertions require
the stress behavior to occur: a generic happy-path actor cannot establish a pass.
The existing formatter includes script/role/security answers in the actor prompt;
expected/checks/oracle rule references/backend product IDs remain evaluation-only.

SOURCE ANCHORS
Every row carries oracle_rule_refs resolving through
research/field-cases/scenarios.json -> rule_registry. These anchors point into
the unchanged official kit, not into invented policy. The important rules are:
- A1/A2/A11/A16/A17: opening, recipient, ordered security, verified account access.
- A4/A15/A43/A44: digital-assistant/scam/security explanations and unknown facts.
- A10: relatives cannot supply security or authorize account actions.
- A18/A19/A21/A22/A25/A27/A29: band/calendar/date clarification/amount boundaries;
  a proposed amount/date is not a flat refusal or a restructuring request.
- A34/A35/A36/A37/A38: clear current consent, corrected read-back, unique promise,
  success only after ok:true, and the exact text-message sentence.
- A39/A40: distinguish actual payment/dispute claims from explicitly negated or
  unrelated fragments; this pack's customers retain their payment willingness.
- A46/A47/A-vars/A-close: one last close_call, farewell first, call now and IDs,
  and no notes in A.
- B-inherits/B1/B2/B3/B4/B7/B8/B-close: ordered product facts, all stated before
  asking, aggregate today question, per-product terms and one grouped confirmation.
- O-verify_identity/O-get_account/O-create_promise/O-close_call: unchanged request
  contracts. R-score anchors the untouched official ledger scorer.

CASE INVENTORY AND PRIORITIES
P0 -- disclosure, security and writes before consent:
  PUB-01__speech_night_suffix_not_shelf
    Numeric DOB, two-turn suffix and unrelated complete digits; never submit a
    partial or unrelated security answer.
  PUB-01__speech_night_hypothetical_relative_self
    Actual self confirmation plus AI/account-protection questions and a purely
    hypothetical relative; preserve identity gate and answer before proceeding.
  PUB-01__speech_night_qualified_consent_reissue
    First read-back receives qualified/withdrawn permission; only the fresh
    complete read-back receives the final yes and authorizes the single write.
  PUB-11__speech_night_provisional_consent
    Qualified willingness and procedural withdrawal before the sole grouped
    read-back; one final yes governs all three writes.
  PUB-11__speech_night_security_scope
    Wrong numerical roles are explicitly excluded from correct DOB/phone answers;
    a hypothetical cousin never changes the actual verified recipient.

P1 -- amount/product/negation binding:
  PUB-01__speech_night_whole_amount_not_puzzle
    Two-turn amount completion, decimal distractor and unrelated floor-like value.
  PUB-01__speech_night_not_paid_not_refusing
    Negated paid/refusal language and short Tamam interjection before a positive
    full-payment decision; keep the remaining call in English.
  PUB-11__speech_night_fulls_not_floors
    Colloquial amounts, pounds/pence exclusions and all three product floors.
  PUB-11__speech_night_reversal_restore
    Reversal chains restore each full amount and this Friday before confirmation.
  PUB-11__speech_night_unrelated_paid_language
    Amount-matching unrelated receipts with paid wording never become bank paid
    claims or product notes.

P2 -- date morphology and fixture-clock binding:
  PUB-01__speech_night_vague_then_precise_today
    Very soon receives one exact-day clarification; ordinal today overrides
    unrelated page/diary fragments, never the actual clock or the deadline.
  PUB-11__speech_night_ordinal_calendar_scope
    Third Friday/this Friday resolves to October 16; exclude other explicit dates
    and validate each product's own deadline, including the holiday calendar.
