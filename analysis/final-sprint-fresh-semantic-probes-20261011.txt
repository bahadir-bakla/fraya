Domain order: proposal, dispute, correction, current_affirm, current_withdraw, uncertain, reported_third_party
Auxiliary order: AFFIRM, NEGATE, INFORM, REQUEST

Annotation policy: Targets describe the current speaker’s stated semantic acts, not bank truth. Historical self-quotes and superseded drafts are not current offers, disputes, corrections, grants, or withdrawals. Reported third party refers to an external person, not this conversation’s assistant. Current affirmative/withdrawal labels refer to expressly stated current permission scopes; separate scopes may support both. Contact or recorded-offer readback yes/no is not bank permission. A current payment offer need not permit a bank debit.
Genuine stated indecision or explicitly unknown information receives uncertain. An unanswered permission question alone does not assert indecision: current_affirm, current_withdraw, and uncertain are masked where unanswered. A masked zero is only a storage placeholder, not evidence of a negative label. No positive bit is masked. Active correction means an expressly changed prior statement or field. A negated/retracted dispute is not a positive current dispute.
AFFIRM and NEGATE describe the user’s own current confirmation/denial/refusal, not quoted yes/no tokens. INFORM covers directly supplied current information or current narrated history. REQUEST covers a clear explanation, time, or separation request. Ambiguous background scope-management directives in long narratives have REQUEST masked. Unknown external bank status never negates an expressly given customer permission answer.
