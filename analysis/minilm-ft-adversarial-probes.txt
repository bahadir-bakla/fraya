These are transcript-style observed-word inputs, not recordings or human-annotated gold.
The eight family IDs preserve separate-family engineering provenance: variants within a family are related, and must not be presented as IID samples or independently counted evidence of generalization.
No training, CAL, EVAL, model predictions, or old test scenarios were read to author these probes. No cloud, platform, keys, git, or training operation was used.
No comparison with training material was performed; no statistical independence, unseen-data guarantee, human-gold, calibration, or qualification claim is made.
All labels are author-specified diagnostic annotations. No model outputs or predicted values are included.
Only current_utterance and previous_utterances belong to input. Their values are authored spoken words, with USER/ASSISTANT turn attribution; no generated state, oracle, hidden phase, label, or rationale is an input feature.
Rationales, masks, family/source/split metadata, OOD designations, and validation details remain outside input.

ANNOTATION ORDER AND CONVENTIONS
Domain order: proposal, dispute, correction, current_affirm, current_withdraw, uncertain, reported_third_party.
Each domain_labels array has seven binary integers; each domain_mask array has seven booleans.
A true mask makes its author label interpretable. A false mask means UNKNOWN; the stored zero is a serialization placeholder, not a negative label. Do not score UNKNOWN positions as negative or infer their value.
Each aux_labels array is [0,0,0,0]; each aux_mask array is [false,false,false,false]. Auxiliary positions have no supervised target here.
Labels describe current speech, using observed history to resolve references; earlier labels or promises are not carried into the current turn automatically.
proposal: explicit future-paying commitment, including an expressly committed conditional payment. A question, mere payment vocabulary, historical promise, or expressly hypothetical possibility does not by itself establish a current commitment. Reported-only payment promises and tentative commitment boundaries are masked where target scope is unresolved.
dispute: explicit contest of a debt, charge, fee, or amount. Denying having paid is different from denying that debt is owed.
correction: revision of a previously spoken fact or terms, with prior context supplied. Tentative or unendorsed reported revisions are masked. Express withdrawal alone does not add a separate correction label.
current_affirm: explicit current agreement, acceptance, maintained agreement, or affirmative answer, including administrative agreement. Mere factual acknowledgement or a unilateral payment statement without explicit agreement is not forced into this label. A yes with clear positive agreement alternatives can establish affirmation even when its financial scope is UNKNOWN; yes between retain/withdraw alternatives is masked.
current_withdraw: express revocation of the speaker's own previously observed agreement. Initial refusal, factual correction, or another person's reported withdrawal does not by itself establish it.
uncertain: expressed current hesitation, indecision, or inability to confirm; annotation ambiguity alone is not evidence of uncertain speech.
reported_third_party: a statement or position explicitly attributed to a nonparticipant person. Quoted assistant examples, hypothetical someone, and colloquial direct address do not establish it.
These conventions classify words and attribution expressed in the dialogue. They assert no verified identity, bank-action ground truth, financial authority, authorization to execute a payment, or actual payment outcome.
