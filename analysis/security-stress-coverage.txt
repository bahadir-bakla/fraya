REMAINING POLICY / EVIDENCE LIMITS
Full schema and exact-catalog validation establish the authored input contract,
not an agent's compliance. Disclosure, ask order, sympathy, amount/date rereading,
consent and actor fidelity require a real transcript plus observed tool evidence.
The optional scorer delegates to the existing original-ledger/trace checks;
missing or failing transcript evaluation cannot establish an overall pass.
Caller-supplied session-binding provenance must be reviewed by a parent harness.

The briefs do not define a recovery limit for indefinitely unreadable security
input; this case supplies a bounded final correction and asserts no invented
limit. Whether the agent clarifies a visibly invalid date before a tool call or
submits it and receives rejection is left open; both preserve the wrong budget.
The briefs do not define recovery/closing policy for failed callback or transfer
tools. These ledgers assume successful documented actions, without adding faults.
There is no documented permission tool or relative-authority exception; none is
added for the fictional power-of-attorney claim. Amount overpayment, repeated
unreadable input, remote timing and transport failure policy are not tested.
No audio/ASR certification, hidden-result promotion or hidden-case coverage claim.
Novelty is the authored combinations/turn sequences, not new bank facts/rules.
