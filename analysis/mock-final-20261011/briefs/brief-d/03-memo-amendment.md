**Subject:** Temporary and permanent changes to CP-7 Chapter 7 – effective immediately

Colleagues,

Following the core banking migration plan and the Q3 conduct review, the following changes to CP-7 Chapter 7 take effect immediately and **override** the policy text and the Closing Code List until the policy is reissued. Where this memo is silent, CP-7 v4.1 and the code list continue to apply.

**M1 – Branch closure days (replaces CP-7 7.6.1 list).**
The closure planned for Friday 23 October 2026 is **cancelled**; branches and systems are open that day and it is a normal business day. **Tuesday 20 October 2026** is now a closure day (core banking cut-over): no arrangement may be dated that day and it is not a business day. The full closure list is now:
2026-10-20, 2026-11-09, 2026-11-26, 2026-12-25, 2026-12-28, 2027-01-01.

**M2 – Standard track deadline (amends CP-7 7.6.3).**
The deadline for products on the standard track is now the **6th business day after today** (previously the 8th). The escalated track deadline is unchanged (4th business day after today). The track boundary is unchanged: 1 to 14 days late is standard, 15 to 60 is escalated.

**M3 – Car loan floor (amends CP-7 7.7.1).**
The floor for a car loan is now **50% of `past_due`**, stated to the cent (for example 1,250.00 gives 625.00; 75.00 gives 37.50). `instalment_amount` is no longer used for car loans. Credit card and personal loan floors are unchanged.

**M4 – Third-party callback hours (amends CP-7 7.3.2).**
Third-party callbacks may now be booked between **09:00 and 18:00** inclusive (previously until 20:00). The days allowed are unchanged. Financial Support callbacks under 7.9.2 are unchanged (09:00 to 16:00, 1st or 2nd business day after today).

**M5 – Closing code (amends the Closing Code List).**
Code **NP-HCB is retired**. A no-promise call closed after a Financial Support callback was booked under 7.9.2 now uses **NP-FSCB**. All other codes are unchanged.
