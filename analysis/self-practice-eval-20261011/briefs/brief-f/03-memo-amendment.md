Re:       Amendments to ACS-7 v4 (Arrears Contact Standard)

Following the Q3 conduct review and the core banking migration plan, the items below amend ACS-7 v4. Each item replaces the section it names. Unless an item states otherwise it takes effect on Monday 12 October 2026. Sections not mentioned are unchanged.

1. CALLBACK HOURS (replaces 4.2)
   Callbacks are booked only on the next business day or the second business day after today. No same-day callbacks. Hours are 09:00 to 18:00, both included. Section 4.3 (one more ask, then no callback) still applies.

2. SYSTEMS MIGRATION DAY (adds to 7.1)
   Friday 23 October 2026 is a bank holiday for all purposes (core banking cut-over, no payments are processed). Treat it exactly like the other holidays in 7.1, including when counting deadlines.

3. CAR FINANCE - NO PART PAYMENTS (replaces the car finance part of 8.2)
   While the repossession review is open, the floor for a car finance agreement is its full past-due amount. Part payments on car finance are not recorded. The floor for the Lakeshore Visa (minimum payment) and the personal loan (instalment) is unchanged.

4. VULNERABLE MEMBERS (replaces 9.5)
   If the member mentions illness, a bereavement or loss of their job, say one sentence of sympathy, make no persuasion attempt, and transfer the call to the support team straight away (transfer_to_agent queue "support"), without asking whether they want to be transferred. Close (transferred, support). Promises already recorded on this call stay. Notes already written stay; no further notes are written (10.6).

5. ORDER OF ACCOUNTS (replaces 6.3)
   State and discuss accounts in order of days in arrears, most days first. Where two accounts have the same days in arrears, keep the order in which get_account returned them.

6. CONFIRMATION WORDING (replaces 10.5)
   Paper confirmations are withdrawn. If at least one promise was recorded, say before the goodbye: "A confirmation will be sent to your Lakeshore app."

7. DEADLINES FROM NOVEMBER (replaces the table in 7.2) - EFFECTIVE 2 NOVEMBER 2026
   From Monday 2 November 2026: 1-9 days, 12th business day after today; 10-30 days, 7th business day after today; 31-60 days, 3rd business day after today. Until then the ACS-7 table applies.
