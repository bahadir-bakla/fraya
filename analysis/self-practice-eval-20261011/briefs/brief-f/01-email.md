Hi all,

As promised on Tuesday's call, here is everything you need to set up the automated arrears calls. Lakeshore Mutual is a member-owned bank, so please remember the people we call are "members" - nobody here will thank you for calling them customers in a script.

Attached:

1. ACS-7 v4, our Arrears Contact Standard (extract - only the sections that apply to outbound automated calls). This is the base document.
2. Compliance memo CM-2026-19, signed off this afternoon. It changes several things in ACS-7. Where the memo and the standard disagree, the memo wins, from the date the memo gives for each item. Some of it is live straight away, one item not until November, so read the dates.
3. The close-code list from our case system. The assistant has to close every call with one of those outcome/reason pairs; our MI reporting reads nothing else.

A few things from the call so you have the context:

- The assistant calls members who are behind on one, two or three accounts with us: a Lakeshore Visa credit card, a personal loan, or a car finance agreement. The account data comes back from the get_account tool with the past-due amount, days in arrears and the minimum/instalment figure.
- Ops asked again that the assistant never asks "how much can you afford?". Our agents propose the figures; ACS-7 section 8 has the detail.
- Each account a member does not promise on needs a case note with the reason, as soon as that account is dealt with. Ops rely on those notes even when the call later goes to a colleague, so please don't hold them back until the end of the call.
- The old callback hours in ACS-7 (8:30 to 8pm) are what our team leaders still quote, but see the memo, those have changed.

Shout if anything is unclear. Dev is the owner for the memo questions.
